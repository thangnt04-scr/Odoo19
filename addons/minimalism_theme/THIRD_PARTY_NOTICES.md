## MuK reference

MuK Web Theme 19.0.1.4.9 was reviewed for behavior and edge cases: dynamic navbar width, constrained screens, field validity, modal sizing and chatter layout. No MuK source code, assets, text or branding is included. The add-on does not depend on MuK and does not implement its appsbar, chatter-resize or dialog-maximize features.
